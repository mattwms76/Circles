# Creator profile — design audit

**Scope:** `/person/1` public creator profile, signed-out captures at 320×740, 390×844, 768×1024, and 1440×1000. Coral/cream/sage identity is consistent. No source, server, account, data, or test-suite changes were made.

## Findings

## Confirmed visual checks

- **Gutters and breakpoints:** 20px mobile/tablet content gutters and 30px desktop main-column gutter are consistent; the 1440px layout cleanly separates 226px navigation, 688px profile column, and 296px community rail. No horizontal overflow is reported (`documentWidth` equals viewport in every profile metric entry; `offscreenText` is empty).
- **Geometry/overlap:** Cover (150px high), overlapping avatar, identity block, action row, Moments pill, and post card have stable relationships across all four sizes. No visible overlap or fixed-header/tab occlusion was found. At 320px the post continues below the viewport as normal scroll content rather than being clipped by a fixed control.
- **Type and wrapping:** Name is a readable 29px/36px hierarchy; bio is 13px/21px and wraps safely in the available column. Handles are 12px. Post metadata and the Slow Living tag use compact 10px text; these are metadata/badge-like treatments and are not counted as body-text failures. The 12px interactive Moments pill remains legible.
- **Touch sizing:** Follow/Say hello are approximately 47–49px high in metrics and meet the 44px goal. The smaller shared IconButton is the exception noted above.
- **Safe areas/header:** On non-wide layouts `AppShell` applies top safe-area inset before its 70px header; desktop uses the 86px header and side rails. The header is outside the ScrollView, so it is not expected to occlude profile content. Keyboard behavior remains unverified except for the source risk above.

## Coverage / caveats

Active conversations/live sessions do not exist for this audit. Signed-out gated states were source-reviewed where applicable and are explicitly not reported as visually verified. No unrelated backend/product audit was performed.
