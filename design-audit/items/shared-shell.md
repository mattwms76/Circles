# Shared typography, controls, shell and tabs

**Scope.** Read-only audit of the assigned shared files against the signed-out captures at 320×740, 390×844, 768×1024 and 1440×1000. The screenshots are sufficient to verify the shared header, mobile tab bar, desktop shell and visible home geometry; other states are source-reviewed only. No source was changed, and no server/browser/test run was performed.

## Shared positives / non-issues

- Gutters are generally coherent: mobile content uses 20 px (`app-shell.tsx:284-286`), wide content uses 30 px with a 32 px header gutter (`app-shell.tsx:85-92, 284-286`), and the wide 1270 px shell is centered. The captures preserve the coral/cream/sage identity without visible horizontal overflow in the assigned home screenshots.
- The desktop sidebar navigation rows are approximately 49 px high (`app-shell.tsx:201-207`) and the primary `Button` is approximately 47 px high (`circles-ui.tsx:168-172`), so they should not be “fixed” as part of the compact-control issues above.
- Safe-area handling is present in both shell top padding and tab-bar bottom padding (`app-shell.tsx:68, 71-76`; `_layout.tsx:14, 33`). The remaining concern is the missing **content** compensation for the tab bar, not an absence of safe-area APIs.
- No fixed header occlusion is visually evident: the header is in normal layout flow and content begins below its border. The wide rail geometry is intentionally dense; compact noninteractive badges and eyebrow labels were not flagged.

## Conditional and unverified states

- The supplied captures are signed-out gates; active conversations and live sessions do not exist in this dataset. Their gated states must be source-reviewed and are **not claimed visually verified** here. `AppShell` exposes `requireAuth/showLogin` (`app-shell.tsx:67, 157, 240-265, 364`) but the login/gate implementation is outside the assigned files.
- No moderation, editing, keyboard-open, or overlay implementation is present in the three assigned files. Those modes are therefore not visually verified in this item; the 44 px and bottom-occlusion fixes should be regression-checked when their dialogs/sheets and keyboard states are audited in their owning screens.

**Conclusion:** the strongest shared causes are the tab-bar/content-height contract and undersized compact Pressables. Address those centrally first; they affect every mobile screen and the wide community rail without changing the visual identity.
