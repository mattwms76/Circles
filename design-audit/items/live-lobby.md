# Live discovery lobby — design audit

**Screen:** `/live` (`app/live/index.tsx`)  
**Captures reviewed:** signed-out/empty lobby at 320×740, 390×844, 768×1024, and 1440×1000. The captures show no active conversations/live sessions.  
**Identity:** coral/cream/sage treatment is consistent across all four sizes.

## Findings

## Cross-size review

- **Gutters:** the mobile/tablet content uses 20 px padding (`app-shell.tsx:282-287`), and the wide content uses 30 px (`app-shell.tsx:282-287`) with the 226 px left rail and 296 px right rail. The screenshots show consistent, non-doubled gutters and no horizontal overflow.
- **Typography:** the 29/36 px title, 13 px supporting copy, 14 px empty text, and 12 px technical note establish a clear hierarchy. The 10 px eyebrow/live badge are compact labels, not body copy; no intentional badge needs to be enlarged.
- **Geometry/occlusion:** the fixed 70 px narrow header and 86 px wide header are visibly clear of the lobby content; wide sidebar/right-rail borders and the central sage notice do not overlap. Safe-area top padding is applied in `AppShell` for non-wide widths (`app-shell.tsx:71-76`).
- **Conditional modes:** loading, API-error, empty, and populated branches are present in `app/live/index.tsx:64-126`. Loading/error/populated cards, long titles, thumbnail failures, and keyboard/camera permission states were not captured and should not be treated as visually verified. No editing or moderation UI is implemented in this assigned lobby file.

**Overall:** clean and cohesive empty lobby across the supplied viewports. The actionable shared issue is the sub-44 px header control box; the main page-specific risks are unverified populated-card geometry and the mobile empty-state spacing.
