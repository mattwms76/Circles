Reproduce the local checks

pnpm check runs tokens, TypeScript and regressions. pnpm test:visual runs the browser suite on its isolated preview server; stop the handoff preview first or use a separate port/config. Android and iOS exports use pnpm export:android and pnpm export:ios.
