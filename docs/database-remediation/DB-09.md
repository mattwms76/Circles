## Guard behavior

Before issuing any drop, the migration requires all four reviewed roles and validates every pair's exact role OID array, command, permissive flag, raw predicate node trees, and normalized expected predicate. It accepts only one of two whole states:

1. all twelve reviewed duplicate policies exist and exactly equal their reviewed survivors; or
2. none of the twelve exists and every survivor has the reviewed definition (the idempotent post-state).

A partial state, missing table/policy, unexpected command/role/predicate, missing RLS, or changed survivor aborts the transaction. The migration has twelve explicit `DROP POLICY IF EXISTS` statements; it deliberately has no generic policy-discovery or dynamic drop loop.

The preflight also confirms the retained native-executor policy shapes (including `circles_admin_native_users_lock_only`), the `circles_app_deny_anon_authenticated` restrictive policy on each affected app table, and that `anon`/`authenticated` lack selected table DML/SELECT/TRUNCATE and private-schema `USAGE`. The policy drops do not issue a `GRANT` or `REVOKE`; thus they do not alter native-executor ACLs or browser privileges. The final post-state check proves every survivor remains exact and each selected duplicate is absent.
